Hydration Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

1. Overview

Hydration is a personal hydration habit tracker. It does not require an account, does not display ads, and does not perform cross-app tracking. The data you enter is used mainly to generate records, progress, statistics, and reminders on your device.

Hydration does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Personal goals and statistics shown in the app are not medical opinions.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers. Information you enter includes drink records (beverage, volume, drinking time, and optional note), daily goal, quick sizes, workdays, active hours, quiet hours, reminder slots, and beverage counting rules.

When the system asks about notification permission, the app requests permission only for local reminders.

3. How We Use Data

This data is used only on your device to calculate daily progress, generate trends and statistics, create local reminders, and export records when you choose to do so. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or behavior analysis.

4. Data Storage and iCloud Sync

By default, your data is stored only on your device. If you tap Sync iCloud in Plan, the app uses CloudKit to sync drink records, plan settings, and reminder states to the private CloudKit database associated with your Apple ID.

iCloud data is stored by Apple's iCloud infrastructure, and the developer cannot access the contents of your private CloudKit database. Sync is not automatic, and you can stop using it at any time.

5. Notifications

Reminders are implemented with iOS local notifications and do not rely on remote push services. Notification content comes from reminders you set in the app and is not sent to the developer.

6. Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. The app does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking. Exported files are handled by the iOS system share sheet, and whether to save or send them is your choice.

7. Data Export and Deletion

You can export JSON or CSV files in Plan > Data Management. Tapping Clear All Data deletes local records, plan, and pending reminders. This action cannot be undone.

Clearing local data does not automatically delete data already synced to iCloud. If you enabled iCloud Sync and want to delete cloud data, manage this app's storage in your Apple ID iCloud settings or contact us through the email below for guidance.

8. Children's Privacy

Hydration is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Policy Changes

If we update this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date on this page. Material changes will be announced in the app or through another reasonable notice.

10. Contact Us
