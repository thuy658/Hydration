Hydration Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

1. Overview

Hydration is a personal hydration habit tracker. Core use does not require an account, and the app does not display ads or perform cross-app tracking. If you choose to create an optional account, your synced data is stored by our sync service provider so it can be restored on another device.

Hydration does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Personal goals and statistics shown in the app are not medical opinions.

2. Information We Collect

Without an account, we do not collect name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers. Information you enter includes drink records (beverage, volume, drinking time, and optional note), daily goal, quick sizes, workdays, active hours, quiet hours, reminder slots, and beverage counting rules. This data is used on your device and is not uploaded.

If you create an optional account, the app sends your chosen username, a password handled by our sync service provider, and the synced records, plan, reminder state, and completion preference to the sync server so your data can be restored and kept up to date.

When the system asks about notification permission, the app requests permission only for local reminders.

3. How We Use Data

This data is used to calculate daily progress, generate trends and statistics, create local reminders, export records when you choose to do so, and synchronize your optional account data between your own devices. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or behavior analysis.

4. Data Storage

Without an account, your data is stored only on your device. With an optional account, records, plan, and reminder state are stored by our sync service provider using HTTPS and per-user access controls. The app does not sync through iCloud or any unrelated cloud service.

5. Notifications

Reminders are implemented with iOS local notifications and do not rely on remote push services. Notification content comes from reminders you set in the app and is not sent to the developer.

6. Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing. A third-party cloud service hosts optional account and sync data as our backend provider. The app does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking. Exported files are handled by the iOS system share sheet, and whether to save or send them is your choice.

7. Data Export and Deletion

You can export JSON or CSV files in Data > Local Data Management. Tapping Clear All Data deletes local records, plan, and pending reminders. This action cannot be undone.

If you created an account, Data > Delete Account deletes the account and all data synced to the server. It does not automatically clear local data on the device, so you can also use Clear All Data if you want to remove the local copy.

8. Children's Privacy

Hydration is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Policy Changes

If we update this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date on this page. Material changes will be announced in the app or through another reasonable notice.

10. Contact Us
